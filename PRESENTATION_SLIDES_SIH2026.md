## 📑 SLIDE 3: Technical Approach & Methodology

### Technologies Used:
* **Frontend & Edge Engine:** Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PWA Offline Service Workers.
* **Computer Vision & Crypto:** HTML5 Canvas, CIEDE2000 Algorithm, WebCrypto API (SHA-256).
* **Database & Synchronization:** Local-First IndexedDB, Supabase PostgreSQL, Row-Level Security (RLS).
* **PDF & GIS Analytics:** jsPDF Form F engine, Leaflet pan-India seizure cluster mapping.

### Implementation Methodology:
* **Step 1 — Interdiction:** Officer targets sample in reticle with live sharpness check.
* **Step 2 — Edge Assay:** CIELAB $\Delta E$ calculates narcotic match in $< 5\text{ms}$ on device.
* **Step 3 — Evidence Sealing:** SHA-256 photo hash, GPS coordinates, and timestamp locked.
* **Step 4 — Panchnama & Sync:** 1-Click Form F PDF generated and synced to Supabase ledger.
